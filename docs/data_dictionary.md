# Data dictionary

Expected contents of `data/` and `outputs/`. Raw ANP/SGB REATE material is **not** committed — fetch it from the public sources cited below.

## `data/raw/` (not versioned)

Original inputs used to build the derived products. Fetch from:

- **ANP** (Agência Nacional do Petróleo, Gás Natural e Biocombustíveis) — [gov.br/anp](https://www.gov.br/anp/): well composite logs, well header coordinates, structural maps.
- **SGB REATE** (Serviço Geológico do Brasil, Repositório de Exploração Brasileiro) — public well files for the Alagoas Sub-basin.
- **IBGE** — urban area polygons (municipality/settlement shapefiles) for NE Brazil.

## `data/derived/` (versioned when small enough)

Author-generated products required by the workflows.

### `data/derived/wells/`
- **`wells_data_corrected.csv`** — per-well lithology intervals, used by W2.
  Columns: `WELL` (string), `FROM` (m, TVDSS), `TO` (m, TVDSS), `LITO` (`Halite`, `Anhydrite`, `Clay`, …), `Ciclos_Evaporiticos` (cycle label or blank).
- **`well_xy.csv`** — well surface-hole coordinates for W1 IDW interpolation.
  Columns: `WELL`, `X` (m, projected), `Y` (m, projected).

### `data/derived/intermediates/`
- **`cycles_data.csv`** — per-well cycle-aggregated properties (top, base, thickness, mean insolubles) regenerated by `run/run_cycles_aggregation.py` from `wells_data_corrected.csv`.

### `data/derived/grids/`
ASCII grids used as inputs to W1 favorability scoring (one per evaporitic cycle).
Filenames follow `config.CYCLE_TO_ASC_PREFIX` — e.g. `depth_topo_Cycle_1.asc`, `fault_distance_Cycle_2b.asc`.

### `data/derived/faults/`
Cycle-specific fault/cycle-boundary shapefiles used to compute distance rasters.

### `data/derived/urban/`
- **`urban_areas.shp`** — urban polygons for the W1 distance-to-urban constraint.

## `data/sample/` (optional)
Lightweight demo inputs that exercise the full pipeline at reduced extent. Not required for reproducing the paper.

## `outputs/` (gitignored by default)

Generated by the runners; structure mirrors the four workflows.

### `outputs/favorability/` (W1)
- `favorability_Cycle_*.asc` — combined 0–10 favorability raster per cycle.
- `scored_*_Cycle_*.asc` — per-criterion intermediate rasters (depth, thickness, insolubles, faults, edges, urban).

### `outputs/cavern_optimization/` (W2)
- `optimal_cavern_intervals_analysis.csv` — single best (well, cycle) configuration.
- `pareto_frontier.csv` — non-dominated solutions over (net volume, insolubles %).

### `outputs/field_layout/` (W3)
- `caverns_pareto_<scenario>.shp` — cavern footprints in EPSG:31985 (SIRGAS 2000 / UTM 25S) per Pareto scenario (`HD_0.75`, `HD_2.00`).

### `outputs/h2_energy/` (W4)
- `h2_capacity_pareto.csv` — per-scenario cavern count, total volume, working mass, total energy (TWh), effective energy (TWh).
